POLYPONG

up to 8 players · one keyboard · shrinking arena
3 lives each. Get scored on three times and you are out.
Arena reshapes as players die: octagon → … → triangle → classic pong.
When you are out you click to drop a black hole into the next arena — it drags the ball in.
Every 3rd elimination drops a sun instead: it shoves the ball away and superheats it. A hot ball costs the next victim 2 lives.
Green dot appears every few rounds — hit it and the ball splits into three.
P or Esc pauses.
